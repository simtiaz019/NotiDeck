# Community code of conduct

Treat contributors and users with respect. Welcome differing experience levels and
technical opinions. Harassment, threats, discrimination, doxxing, and sharing private
credentials or personal information are not acceptable. Critique ideas constructively.

Maintainers may remove abusive contributions, restrict participation, or ban repeat
violators. Decisions should be proportionate and explained privately when appropriate.
Report concerns privately to a repository maintainer through the hosting platform;
this new project has not yet designated a public contact address. Do not post private
reports in public issues. Maintainers must disclose conflicts and recuse themselves
from handling reports about their own conduct.
